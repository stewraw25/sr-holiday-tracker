How the rules work

📅
Holiday year
Runs 1 April to 31 March, in line with your accounts.
🏦
Bank holidays
England & Wales dates are worked out automatically for every year and never come out of anyone's allowance.
⚖️
UK minimum
Full-time staff get at least 20 days plus the 8 bank holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over.
